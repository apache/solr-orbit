Initial port of [OpenSearch Benchmark](https://github.com/opensearch-project/opensearch-benchmark) to Apache Solr. Solr Orbit provisions SolrCloud clusters from a distribution, from sources or in Docker, runs workloads against Solr through the Collections API, `/update` and `/select`, collects JVM, node, collection, query, indexing and cache telemetry, and keeps metrics on the local filesystem. The `convert-workload` command translates an OpenSearch Benchmark workload, including its query DSL and index mappings, to the Solr format. (@janhoy)
