`convert-workload` translates OpenSearch workloads more faithfully. Exclusive range bounds stay exclusive, date bounds with a space separator and whole-day `lte` bounds convert correctly, a `date_histogram` `fixed_interval` maps to the equivalent Solr gap instead of defaulting to `+1MONTH`, and the index-stats merge-wait idiom becomes Solr Orbit's `wait-for-merges` operation.
